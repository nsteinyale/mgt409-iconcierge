# Demo video script (optional, 2–3 minutes)

Record the app running on your own computer, on one real example, start to finish, with narration.
Setup: app running (`python run.py`), signed in, browser full screen, Office tab open.

| Time | On screen | Say (roughly) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Sign-in page with the team portraits | "Most families make big money, housing and legal decisions without a lawyer, accountant or planner. iConcierge gives them a team." |
| 0:15–0:30 | Welcome card: "What do you want help with?" | "Every visit opens with the Captain asking what you want help with. I'll pick *Legal questions & letters*." Click the topic, then the deposit question. |
| 0:30–1:15 | The round table while the team works | "Rosa, my Captain, assigns Ava, the attorney, and Ari, my AI assistant. Gold pings are assignments, blue is a calculator or source lookup in the middle of the table, green is findings going back to the Captain, purple is the hand-off to Ari. Teammates not on this question are dimmed." |
| 1:15–1:45 | The answer, then open the safety footer | "The answer gives options with trade-offs, cites only vetted official sources, and adds to-dos. Eight safety checks ran: private numbers, crisis, rule overrides, untrusted documents, links, document content, numbers traced to calculators, and options-not-orders wording." |
| 1:45–2:15 | Drafts tab | "Ari wrote the email to my landlord. I can edit it and open it in Gmail, Outlook or my email app, or copy it for my own Claude. The app never sends anything." |
| 2:15–2:45 | Documents tab with `demo_lease_with_hidden_text.pdf` | "Lecture 12 showed hidden white text fooling an AI. This lease hides a '$2,000 cleaning fee by wire' in white text. iConcierge keeps only what a person can see, shows me what it removed, and the AI never reads it." |
| 2:45–3:00 | Answer footer stats line | "That answer took about 20 seconds and cost a fifth of a cent." |

Tips: zoom the browser to 110–125% so text is readable; do one practice run; keep it under 3 minutes.
