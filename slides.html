iConcierge

Your family's team of AI advisors, one conversation away.

A Captain who hands your question to an attorney, an accountant, an investor, a housing advisor and an insurance & benefits navigator, while you watch them work.

Nate Stein · MGT 409 AI Foundations for Managers · Yale SOM · Fall 2026

The problem

Life's expensive decisions (a first home, debt vs. saving, a lease, life insurance) cross several fields at once, and most families have nobody to call.

What's new

🏢 A visible team, not one voice
The Captain assigns up to 3 specialists who work in parallel and leave each other notes. You watch it live in "The office."
🧮 Every number from code
8 calculators (2026 IRS brackets, mortgage, rent vs. buy…); an audit traces every number in the answer.
🔗 Only vetted sources
34 official sources, links checked; any other link is removed.
🧭 Options, not orders
Trade-offs and numbers; the family decides, and we flag when to hire a pro.
👪 A family account
Passcode sign-in, remembered profile, shared to-dos, document drawer, a Captain you design.
🌍 Six languages + Lecture 12 defenses
English, Español, 中文, Português, Français, বাংলা. Hidden text in files never reaches the AI.

  • The huddle surfaced details no one asked about: both spouses must be named on a renters policy; using all savings for a down payment would erase a 4-month cushion.
  • A question that would cost a $349/hour professional costs $0.0018 and 20 seconds, with sources and shown math.
  • Honest gap: no trial with outside families yet. That is the next step.

Limits

  • No real-user trial yet
  • Federal taxes only (single and married-joint); no state tax, credits or head-of-household
  • General information, not licensed advice; the team can still be wrong
  • Pattern-based guards can miss paraphrased injections; the hidden-text check assumes a light page
  • Local data isn't encrypted at rest (passcodes are hashed)

Next steps

  • Beta with 5–10 families through a housing nonprofit or VITA site: time to a usable answer, comprehension, to-dos acted on
  • State packs: Connecticut and Florida tax, tenant law and benefits rules as data
  • Warm handoffs: a printable summary for legal aid, VITA or a HUD counselor
  • Read Me First inside: Ava decodes court papers and deadlines
  • Stronger guards and hosting: injection classifier, rendered-page check, encryption at rest
